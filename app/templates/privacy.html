Privacy Statement

Last updated: May 2025

Stock Sentinel is a personal portfolio tool. This statement explains what personal data the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

What data is collected

  • Email address — provided when you sign in via email OTP or a social provider (Google, GitHub, etc.).
  • Display name — your name or username as returned by your identity provider, or your email address if no name is available.
  • Portfolio data — stock tickers, cost basis, prices, and notes that you enter manually.

Why it is collected

  • Your email and display name are used solely to identify your account and associate your portfolio data with you.
  • The site owner may receive a one-time email notification when a new account is created, containing your email address and display name, so the owner knows someone new has joined.

How it is stored

  • Authentication is handled by Supabase. Your credentials are never stored directly by this app.
  • Your email, display name, and portfolio data are stored in a PostgreSQL database managed by Supabase.
  • Data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for marketing or analytics purposes.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting the site owner at the address below.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To do so, contact the site owner directly.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, email admin@stocksentinal.com.
